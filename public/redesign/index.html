VECTOR • INSTITUTIONAL GEX
Dealer Gamma Exposure & Wall Absorption

Tracks market maker delta-hedging obligations across index option surfaces in real time. When net dealer gamma is strongly positive, automated entry gates veto momentum breakout setups and favor mean-reversion absorption.

Deterministic state machine with write-ahead logging ensuring instant fail-closed state recovery upon telemetry interruption.

Regulatory & Operational Scope Notice: TradeBot Sentinel is an engineering research and market microstructure observability framework developed by Aixion Lab. It does not provide investment advice, trading tips, portfolio management, or public financial recommendations under SEBI regulations.
