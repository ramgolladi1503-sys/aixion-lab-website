WHY THIS MATTERS

Why investigate it?

Pattern descriptions are easy to overfit after seeing what happened. The research question is whether the structure exists before outcome knowledge is allowed in.

OBSERVATION

What was seen?

Repeated market-state interactions can look compelling visually even when they do not survive a governed producer/evidence boundary.

HYPOTHESIS

What is being tested?

A frozen structural interaction may improve candidate quality if it survives exact producer authority, recurrence and holdout checks.

RESULT / STATE

Where it stands

Hypothesis frozen. Work remains constrained by producer/evidence authority rather than being forced into a positive result.

METHOD

How the study stays bounded

  1. Freeze the mechanism
  2. Map exact producer authority
  3. Evaluate only admissible real-corpus candidates
  4. Reject unsupported producer assumptions
  5. Preserve the frozen hypothesis if authority is missing

BOUNDARIES

What this page will not claim

  • No invented producer
  • No backfilled authority
  • No holdout access before the gate
  • No claim that a frozen hypothesis is a validated edge

NEXT STEP
