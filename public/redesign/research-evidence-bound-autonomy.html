RESEARCH NOTE / Autonomous systems

Evidence-bound autonomy

How can agent actions remain useful while preserving explicit policy, evidence and authority boundaries?

QUESTION / METHOD / RESULT

Keep the reasoning attached to the result.

The note gives the reader enough context to understand why the work exists and where its conclusions stop.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Why investigate it?

Agent systems become harder to trust when planning, tools, evidence and authority collapse into one opaque action loop.

OBSERVATION

What was seen?

Autonomous capability improves quickly, while reliable boundaries around context, tool calls, evidence and approvals remain a separate engineering problem.

HYPOTHESIS

What is being tested?

Agents can remain useful while explicit policy, evidence and human/system authority boundaries are preserved as first-class state.

RESULT / STATE

Where it stands

Active research supporting the Aixion Control Core MVP architecture.

METHOD

How the study stays bounded

  1. Model intent and context separately
  2. Expose planner/tool transitions
  3. Capture evidence before authority changes
  4. Keep policy evaluation explicit
  5. Design human approval as a system state rather than a UI afterthought

BOUNDARIES

What this page will not claim

  • Demo traces are labelled demonstrations
  • No fake production telemetry
  • No hidden tool authority
  • No unsupported autonomy claims

NEXT STEP

Turn the architecture into an inspectable end-to-end execution trace with explicit policy and evidence stages.

The current state stays attached to the evidence until the next gate is met.
