ENGINEERING STORY

Problem → challenge → build → outcome

The work is explained through its constraints and evidence, not a technology-logo wall.

PROBLEM

What makes this difficult?

Intraday market signals are noisy, while live data can fail in ways that a simple connected/disconnected flag cannot explain.

CHALLENGE

What must remain controlled?

Separate data truth, research output, risk and execution authority while preserving reproducible evidence.

WHAT EXISTS

The system response

A governed market-intelligence and validation system spanning real-time data, research candidates, risk controls and read-only/live observation.

OUTCOME

What this enables

A platform capable of structured research, evidence capture and bounded decision support without publishing proprietary signal logic.

Live data integrity

Connection state, freshness, subscription truth and evidence state are treated as different concerns rather than one green light.

Research authority

Promising research does not silently become trading authority. Candidates remain isolated until explicit validation and governance gates pass.

Human authority

Evidence and decision support can be automated while execution authority remains an explicit boundary.

RESEARCH BOUNDARY

Research is a gated lifecycle, not a shortcut to authority.

The public page explains method without publishing proprietary strategy mechanics.

METHOD

Observe → hypothesize → freeze → test

Candidate logic is frozen before evaluation so results cannot silently rewrite the question being tested.

BOUNDARY

Holdout and live observation stay separate

Research evidence can support a decision to continue testing; it does not automatically grant execution authority.

DATAFeed health split into connection, freshness and subscription truth
RESEARCHResearch candidates isolated behind explicit validation gates
LIVERead-only observation used to expose failure paths before authority
