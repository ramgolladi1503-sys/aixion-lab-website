# Motion Lab — Scene 2: Engineering Intelligence

## Visual target

The arrival sculpture moves inward and loses its closed-loop silhouette. Twelve points begin at positions sampled from that same folded form, then travel outward into a readable, asymmetrical engineering graph. Fine connections appear between those moving points, exposing branches and cross-links rather than a generic glowing network. The camera stays composed and steady while the object itself resolves. Muted bronze, warm paper, mineral teal and restrained rose distinguish groups without turning the scene into a dashboard.

The semantic copy describes observable state, explicit boundaries and evidence. The section is ordinary HTML over the shared scene. Progress is driven by native scroll and reverses along the same interpolation path.

## Constraints

- Continue the same Canvas and render loop from Scene 1; do not mount a second renderer or use a photograph crossfade.
- Derive graph start points from the Scene 1 curve and keep node count, connection topology and positions deterministic.
- Keep line/node count bounded and use instancing for repeated nodes.
- Do not display invented metrics, fake runtime telemetry or unsupported system claims.
- Keep the scene legible without WebGL through semantic copy and the existing static illustration.
