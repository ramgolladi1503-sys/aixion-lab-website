## Reference and acceptance bounds

The rendered site was compared against Direction A for warm canvas, material imagery, whitespace, restrained indigo, balanced typography, calm project presentation and cross-page continuity. Mountains/travel aesthetics, cyber/neon styling, generic AI gradients, glassmorphism-heavy treatment and dashboard-first presentation are not used as the site's visual identity.

The qualitative visual scores and their screenshot basis are recorded in `docs/website/VISUAL_SCORECARD.md`. They are implementation-agent review scores, not independent design research or user-study results.

No browser or visual test was removed or weakened to obtain the passing state. Earlier failed states were treated as regressions and repaired before acceptance.

## Performance and scope limits

The site uses static export, local subset fonts and SVG/editorial imagery without WebGL. Local performance measurements in the generated visual bundle are development observations, not field performance or Lighthouse certification. The intentional first introduction can be skipped and reduced-motion behavior is supported.

Contact prepares an email draft addressed to the public contact email; no backend delivery service exists. Résumé is a printable engineering profile with LinkedIn for employment history. Project claims use public evidence and do not certify current live-runtime health, current project-wide test totals, profitable strategies or enterprise maturity.

Automated browser coverage is Chromium at the specified widths, not a substitute for independent physical-device testing or formal WCAG certification.
