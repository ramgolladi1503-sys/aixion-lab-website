## Evidence and bounds

The `artifacts/visual` bundle contains page renders at 390, 430, 768, 1280 and 1440 pixels, individual motion states, drawer/menu/contact states and local performance JSON. Top, middle and bottom review sheets supplement individual captures. Full-page screenshots do not reproduce sticky positioning at every scroll location, so separate native-scroll screenshots and interaction checks were used.

The tablet hero, About groups, dialog focus, moving-text contrast and entry material were repaired after inspection. Scores are the current rendered assessment, not retroactively assigned to the earlier failed versions. Final assembled regression passed all 59 checks. A subsequent tablet review found the new anchored rail too tall at 768px; the final repair uses a vertical narrative through tablet widths. The repaired rendering was inspected at full-page and readable-detail scales before closing this assessment.

Remaining release limitations: email uses a draft handoff rather than a backend delivery service; résumé is a printable public profile with LinkedIn for employment history; external project documentation is not a live-runtime certificate. No deployment or main merge was performed.
